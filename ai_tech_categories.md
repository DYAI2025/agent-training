### 7. **Robotics/IoT** (Niedrigere Priorität)

- **Autonomous Systems** (Self-driving, Drones, Autonomous Delivery)
- **IoT Platforms** (Device Management, Data Collection, Edge Processing)

### 8. **AI SaaS Applications** (Hohe Priorität)
